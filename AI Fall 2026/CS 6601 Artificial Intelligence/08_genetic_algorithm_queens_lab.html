The setup

A population is just several candidate answers

Instead of improving one board, the genetic algorithm starts with four different boards. The digit strings are compressed board descriptions.

!
Plain English: four searchers begin in four different parts of the solution space.
Connection to the previous lesson

What changed from hill climbing and annealing?

All three optimize a score. The difference is how many candidates they keep and how they explore.

Hill climbingOne candidate; accept only improvement
AnnealingOne candidate; sometimes accept worse
Genetic algorithmMany candidates; select and mix them
