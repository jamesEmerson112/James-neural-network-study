Why gradient descent was mentioned

All three make local improvements

They differ mainly in how a neighboring solution and movement direction are chosen.

Hill climbing
Try neighboring states; keep only an immediate improvement. No derivative required.
Gradient descent
For differentiable continuous problems, use the slope to reduce a loss. It is a directed local method.
Annealing
Try a random neighbor and sometimes keep a worse one. Temperature controls how adventurous it is.
